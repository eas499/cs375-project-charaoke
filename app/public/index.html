How to Play:

  1. Click "Log In" to sign into Spotify. A Spotify Premium account is required to hear audio in game. The game is still playable without logging into Spotify.
  2. Go to "Play game!" and select or search for a song
  3. Once you select a song, start typing in time with the lyrics. The red progress bar helps tell you how much time you have
  4. After you finish your song, you can optionally enter your name if you'd like to put your score on the leaderboard for that song!
